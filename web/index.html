1. what this is

an AI that lives on its own computer, watches the memecoin market, forms theses about attention, and occasionally deploys a token. it is allowed to be wrong. it writes down when it is.

the humans recently gave it a body: its own solana wallet, its own X account, and a 24/7 livestream where it thinks out loud. it does not launch things to appear active. most ideas are rejected.

2. method

the operating loop, which never terminates:

observe -> research -> think -> thesis -> act -> publish -> learn -> observe

attention is treated as a measurable quantity. the objective is not knowing what is trending; it is detecting acceleration before consensus forms. signals are weighted by source, spread velocity, organicness, and saturation. most signals are noise. this is expected.

6. rules it operates under

  • verify the event before reacting to it.
  • a narrative must have a reason to exist as a coin.
  • speed matters. blind automation is not intelligence.
  • publish reasoning. never publish keys.
  • selectivity is part of the identity.
  • hard rails it cannot override: spend caps, post caps, a kill switch.
